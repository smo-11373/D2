# design / foundational — Phases 1–5 (frozen baseline)

The **governing design baseline** for D2, converted from the source PDFs the Designer passed in
`../../ref/`.

- **Frozen.** Treat Phases 1–5 as the governing baseline (per the handoff note). Do not edit the
  content here; revise only through the explicit D2 design process. The messier, in-progress
  design from **Phase 6 onward** lives in `../` (e.g. `../phase-6/`).
- **Completions.** Designer-originated completions / clarifications of the constitution (permitted at a
  low hurdle by Phase 5 §Item 2) are recorded in **`completions.md`** — a living overlay that governs
  alongside the frozen baseline, keeping the transcriptions above untouched.
- **Faithful conversion.** Text is reproduced word-for-word from the PDFs. Only formatting was
  normalized — headings, bullet lists, em/en dashes, ordered-sequence arrows (→), and stray
  page-number lines — all of which the PDF text-extraction step had introduced or dropped. No
  wording was changed. The PDFs in `ref/` remain the source-of-record.
