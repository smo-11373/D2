## C-2026-07-19-1 — The D2 Assistant is a Human-Position-First position

**Completes:** Phase 5 §Item 3 (Human Position First). **Reconciles with:** Phase 2 §Principle 3 and
Phase 4 §Item 3 (the unified interaction point); glossary `d2-assistant`.

**Completion.** The **D2 Assistant** is a first-class **Human-Position-First position** — the D1
Designer's single, unified point of contact with the entire D2 system. It is designed as a *position*
first (a conceptual responsibility boundary with defined inputs, information, authority, and a result it
must produce) and **occupied by a D2 agent**; non-human but treated position-first, exactly as Item 3
prescribes ("an agent may later occupy a position"). It stands among the named positions alongside the
D1 Designer, Design Node Builder, D1 Programmer, D1 / D0 Technical Managers, and D0 Operator.

Its **responsibility**: **conduct the design on the Designer's behalf and answer his queries**,
**interpret and route his input** to the right D2 function while **preserving interaction context**, and
**present his output** (completion reports, Review Stops, Clarification Requests, human-readable
summaries, with drill-down). D2 owns this interaction burden so the Designer never addresses an internal
node, governing authority, or service directly.

**Basis:** the interaction front performs real work (routing, presenting, answering) that no other named
position owns — a position whose work orphans when folded is a real position; and treated position-first,
its needs *demand* part of the D2 surface (Human Position First → Role → Action → Capability).

**Provenance.** Designer-originated completion — **D2 Designer (R-00), 2026-07-19**; low hurdle
(Phase 5 §Item 2), consistent with Phase 2 §Principle 3, Phase 4 §Item 3, glossary `d2-assistant`.
